## LTvLauncher v2026.11.23

### Added
- **What's new dialog (#185)**: after an update, a compact dialog lists the changes once, with a Support us button and Got it. Fresh installs show nothing.

### Changed
- **Compact home status bar (#184)**: the status bar is shorter, leaving more room for your apps.
- **Settings panel (#184, #187)**: the panel slides in with a fading scrim, and focused tiles are tinted. Its width is 360dp.
- **Applications tabs (#184)**: tabs fade between states without flicker, load on first visit and keep your place when moving left and right.

### Fixed
- **Clipped tile shadow (#186)**: the focused tile's shadow is no longer cut off at the left and top edge of the home list. Tiles scrolled sideways now reach the screen edge.

### Maintenance
- **README redesign (#183)**: a compact layout that is easier to scan.
